# The Ultimate Real Estate CRM

**Product vision:** An AI-powered real estate operating system that helps agents acquire leads, build client relationships, market properties, manage showings, close transactions, and generate repeat business.

> Design principle: The platform should do useful work for agents, not merely store data. High-impact actions such as outreach, pricing changes, advertising spend, and contractual steps require appropriate approvals.

# Product Rules

- **Data permissions first:** MLS access, listing syndication, public records, and third-party portal integrations must follow applicable licenses and terms.
- **Consent by design:** Respect opt-in/opt-out requirements, calling and texting restrictions, recording laws, and channel-specific messaging rules.
- **Fair housing:** Avoid discriminatory targeting, unlawful housing preferences, and unjustified sensitive-attribute inference.
- **Humans approve consequential actions:** Do not autonomously execute contracts, change listing prices, spend ad budgets, or send high-risk communications without suitable authorization.
- **AI should explain itself:** Show evidence, uncertainty, and source dates for lead scoring, valuation, comps, and forecasts.
- **Multi-tenant security:** Isolate brokerage data and maintain durable audit trails.

**Positioning:** Not another CRM. A complete real estate revenue and operations platform powered by an accountable AI workforce.
